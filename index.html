Why it's called Holding Good

Some things are worth holding on to. A voice you don't want to forget. A craft that took years to learn. A promise you make to the people who trust you.

To hold good is to keep what matters, and to keep your word.

Holding Good is where I look after ideas until they're ready — giving each one room, time, and care before it goes out on its own. Not a fund. Not a factory. A house.
